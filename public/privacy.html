Privacy & Data Protection Notice

How Manlung Recovery collects, uses, protects, shares and retains personal data when you use our website, client portal and recovery-support services.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who this notice is for: visitors, clients, applicants, people who communicate with Manlung Recovery, and individuals whose information is included in a lawful recovery or investigation matter. This notice should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Who we are

For this website and the services described here, Manlung Recovery is the organisation responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing personal data under applicable law. Privacy enquiries can be sent to manlungrecovery@outlook.com.

Where a third party processes personal data on our instructions, it acts as a service provider/data processor subject to applicable contractual and legal safeguards. This notice does not itself constitute a certificate of registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

2. Kenyan legal framework

Our privacy practices are designed with reference to Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019), the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021, and the Data Protection (Complaints Handling and Enforcement) Regulations, 2021, together with other applicable law and ODPC guidance.

The Act and regulations require lawful, fair and transparent processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, appropriate security and safeguards for international transfers. They also provide data-subject rights and mechanisms for complaints and enforcement.

4. How we use personal data

  • Create and secure client accounts.
  • Authenticate clients and protect access to their cases.
  • Receive, organise, investigate and track recovery requests.
  • Communicate case updates and support responses.
  • Operate Call Admin and other communications features.
  • Secure the website, detect abuse, prevent fraud and investigate security incidents.
  • Process payments where applicable.
  • Comply with legal obligations, lawful requests and regulatory requirements.
  • Maintain appropriate business and security records.

5. Lawful basis

Depending on the activity and circumstances, processing may rely on consent, performance of a contract or steps requested before a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of vital interests, legitimate interests where permitted, or another lawful basis recognised by Kenyan law. Where consent is the basis, we will provide appropriate information about the purpose, data involved, sharing, transfers and withdrawal of consent.

6. Client accounts and social sign-in

You may create an account with email/password or, where enabled, continue with Google or GitHub. When social sign-in is used, the provider supplies identity information such as the account email and profile information permitted by the provider. We use that information to create or authenticate the corresponding Manlung Recovery client account.

Google and GitHub have their own privacy practices. Social authentication does not give Manlung Recovery access to your social account password.

7. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose or make personal data available where reasonably necessary to provide the service or where required or permitted by law, including to contracted technology providers, authentication/cloud-storage providers, communications providers, payment providers, professional advisers, investigators or relevant authorities.

Where disclosure to law enforcement or another authority is required by a valid legal process or is otherwise permitted by law, we may comply. We may also share information where necessary to protect rights, safety, prevent fraud or investigate serious misuse.

8. International transfers

Some technology providers used by an online service may process data outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we will use the safeguards and lawful transfer mechanisms required by the Data Protection Act and applicable regulations, such as appropriate safeguards, an adequacy decision, necessity or valid consent where applicable. Information about material third-party processing can be requested from our privacy contact.

9. Security safeguards

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks, including access control, authentication, MFA for applicable administrative accounts, rate limiting, secure headers, protected administrative sessions, input validation, controlled evidence access and security monitoring/logging.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should never disclose your password, MFA code, recovery code or private key to another person. Report suspected compromise promptly.

10. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, resolving disputes, maintaining security, meeting legal obligations and preserving legitimate business records. Retention periods may differ by data type and case.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements and technical feasibility.

11. Your data-subject rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations in Kenyan law, you may have rights to:

  • be informed about processing of your personal data;
  • access personal data held about you;
  • request correction/rectification of inaccurate or misleading data;
  • object to processing in appropriate circumstances;
  • request erasure/deletion where the law permits;
  • request restriction of processing where applicable;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with the ODPC.

To make a request, email manlungrecovery@outlook.com. We may need reasonable information to verify the identity and authority of the requester before disclosing or changing personal data.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can investigate and respond. You may also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) or use its complaint process where you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed.

13. Personal-data breaches

We maintain procedures for identifying, assessing, containing and responding to security incidents. Where a personal-data breach meets the statutory threshold for notification and presents a real risk of harm, Kenyan law requires the data controller to notify the Data Commissioner without delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, with communication to affected data subjects where required by law. Processors have corresponding obligations to notify the controller without delay and, where reasonably practicable, within 48 hours.

14. Cookies and local browser storage

Manlung Recovery uses strictly necessary browser storage and, where applicable, cookies to operate the website, maintain secure authentication and administrative sessions, protect accounts, remember the cookie-consent choice, and support core features. Some preferences, such as theme selection, may also be stored locally on your device.

The cookie preference panel separates strictly necessary storage from optional preferences, analytics and marketing categories. Strictly necessary storage is always active because the website and account features cannot operate securely without it. Optional categories are off unless you choose to enable them. Optional analytics and advertising tools are not currently activated by the cookie-consent manager; saving an optional choice does not itself activate a third-party service.

You can open Cookie settings at any time to review or change your choice. Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days, or until the consent notice is materially updated. Clearing browser storage may remove the saved choice and may sign you out or remove saved preferences. Blocking required storage can prevent some features from working.

We do not use browser storage to request or store your passwords in plain text. For questions about cookies or privacy, contact manlungrecovery@outlook.com.

15. Children's data

The services are not designed to encourage children to create accounts independently. Where a case concerns a child or includes a child's personal data, the person submitting it must have appropriate authority and must provide only information necessary for the lawful purpose. We will apply additional safeguards where required by Kenyan law and applicable ODPC guidance.

16. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top will show when the notice was last revised. Material changes will be presented in a manner appropriate to the circumstances.

17. Contact

Privacy and data-protection requests: manlungrecovery@outlook.com.

Community safety

Manlung Members Community Privacy and Safety

The Manlung Members community is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Do not use the community if you are under 18. Age eligibility should be confirmed during account registration before access is enabled.

Community members may choose whether their profile picture is public or private. When public, the picture may be visible to other registered members. Choosing private hides the picture from other members; your account name and community actions may still be visible where needed to operate the service.

Status views may show the status owner who viewed their status. Comments on a status may be delivered to the status owner's inbox. Messages, profile information, group posts, and status interactions should be shared thoughtfully.

Manlung Members is for friendly conversation, love, and mutual support. Hate speech, harassment, threats, and discriminatory abuse are not allowed. Main-app administrators may add or remove members and take moderation action for rule violations.

Leaving a group removes your membership. Rejoining may be subject to group access rules. Avoid posting passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information in chats or statuses.

For privacy requests or concerns, contact Manlung Recovery through the contact details published on the Manlung Recovery website.
