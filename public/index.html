Scammed? You are not alone.

Act quickly to protect your accounts, preserve evidence and report the incident. No legitimate service can guarantee that stolen money will be recovered.

01 Contact your bank

Call using the number in the official app, card or website. Report the transaction and ask whether it can be stopped, recalled or disputed.

02 Secure your accounts

Change exposed passwords from a trusted device, enable two-factor authentication, and never share PINs or one-time codes.

03 Save the evidence

Keep transaction IDs, receipts, phone numbers, usernames, URLs, emails, chat screenshots and dates.

04 Report the incident

Report suspected crime to the police/DCI and use official Kenyan cyber-incident reporting channels.

Beware of recovery scams

  • Guaranteed recovery promises or unexpected messages after a loss.
  • Advance fees, release charges, taxes or deposits demanded before recovery.
  • Requests for your banking login, M-Pesa PIN, one-time password, seed phrase or remote device access.
  • Impersonation of government agencies, police, banks, lawyers or past victims.
