Social Media & Email Recovery

Lost access to a social media page or email account? Manlung Recovery provides practical, step-by-step support for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Gmail, and other supported services. We help you understand official recovery routes, secure linked contact details, organize evidence, report impersonation or suspicious activity, and strengthen account security after access is restored.

We never ask for passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or payment PINs. We do not bypass platform security, and account restoration is controlled by the platform and cannot be guaranteed.

What support includes

  • Review available official account recovery options.
  • Guidance for platform recovery forms and support channels.
  • Secure linked email, phone number, active sessions, and two-step verification.
  • Organize evidence and report impersonation or unauthorized access.
  • Account-hardening recommendations after access is restored.

Support is advisory. We do not bypass security controls or access accounts without authorization.
