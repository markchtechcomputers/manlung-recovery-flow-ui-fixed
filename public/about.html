Who We Are

Manlung Tech City is a cyber recovery and digital investigation service. We help individuals and organizations recover lost or stolen devices, regain control of compromised accounts, investigate online scams and fraud, and assess network security — combining technical tracing, platform coordination, and (where needed) law enforcement liaison.

Device Recovery

GPS/IMEI tracing, carrier coordination, and remote lock/wipe guidance for lost or stolen phones, laptops, tablets, and smartwatches.

Identity Theft

Account recovery support, documentation help for banks and platforms, and guidance on locking down further exposure.

Scam Investigation

Evidence collection, payment-trail tracing across banks/mobile money/crypto, and direct engagement with providers and platforms.

Security Assessment

Vulnerability scanning, configuration review, and a prioritized remediation report for organizations.

Our Approach

Every case starts with a structured intake — the same form you'll find under "New Request" — so nothing critical gets missed. From there, a case is reviewed, an investigator is assigned, and you can track progress in real time using your case ID. We keep you updated at each meaningful stage, and every file you submit is stored securely and only accessible to you and the assigned investigator.
