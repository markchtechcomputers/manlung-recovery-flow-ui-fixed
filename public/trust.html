What happens after you submit a request?

01

Sign in and submit your case

Use the client portal to describe what happened and provide only information relevant to the issue. You need a client account to submit a recovery request.

02

We review the information

The case is reviewed to understand the issue, whether it fits the services available, and what clarification or evidence may be needed. A request is not a guarantee that the case will be accepted or resolved.

03

Understand the scope and cost

Before any separately charged work begins, ask for written confirmation of the work proposed, what it includes and excludes, the fee in Kenyan shillings (KES), any third-party charges, and the expected deliverables.

04

Follow updates through your case

For an accepted case, use the case portal and case ID to review available updates and communications. We may recommend contacting your bank, mobile-money provider, online platform, device provider or relevant authorities when appropriate.

Case submission and tracking, plus the currently available Call Admin feature, are described in our Terms as free features unless the website says otherwise.

Before paying: get the exact scope, total fee, any additional charges, payment recipient and applicable terms in writing. If a price or deliverable is unclear, pause and ask us to clarify it. Do not pay based only on pressure or a promise of guaranteed recovery.

There is no universal price for every possible investigation. Any paid work must be quoted and agreed separately before it begins; do not assume that an enquiry or case submission means you have accepted a paid service.

Recovery is not guaranteed

We cannot guarantee the recovery of stolen money, cryptocurrency, digital assets, accounts or devices. Results may depend on evidence, time elapsed, account or platform policies, financial institutions, telecommunications providers, legal restrictions and other factors outside our control.

Tracing, reviewing evidence, sending a report or submitting a request to a provider does not mean that funds or assets have been recovered. We will not promise a result simply to persuade you to pay.

We do not need your secrets

Never share your password, M-Pesa PIN, banking PIN, one-time password (OTP), cryptocurrency seed phrase or private key. Do not give anyone remote access to your device or account just because they claim they can recover it.

Use the official portal for case-specific information and evidence. Share only information relevant to the case. If an account or payment method may be compromised, contact the provider through its official app, website or published support channel as soon as possible.
