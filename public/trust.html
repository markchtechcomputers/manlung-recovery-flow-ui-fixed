The process

What happens after you submit a request?

Here is what to expect, from your first request to case updates.

01

Submit your case

Sign in to the client portal and describe what happened. Share only information relevant to the issue. A client account is required to submit a recovery request.

02

We review the information

We assess the issue, whether it fits the services available, and what clarification or evidence may be needed. A request does not guarantee acceptance or a particular outcome.

03

Agree on the scope and cost

Before separately charged work begins, request written confirmation of the proposed work, what is included and excluded, the fee in Kenyan shillings (KES), any third-party charges and expected deliverables.

04

Follow case updates

For an accepted case, use the client portal and case ID to view available updates. Where appropriate, we may recommend contacting your bank, mobile-money provider, platform, device provider or relevant authorities.

Before you pay: get the proposed work, total fee, any additional charges, payment recipient and applicable terms in writing. If anything is unclear, pause and ask. Never pay because someone pressures you or promises guaranteed recovery.

There is no single price for every possible investigation. Any paid work must be quoted and agreed separately before it begins. Submitting an enquiry or case does not, by itself, mean you have accepted a paid service.

Realistic expectations

Recovery is never guaranteed

We cannot guarantee the recovery of stolen money, cryptocurrency, digital assets, accounts or devices. Outcomes may depend on the available evidence, time elapsed, provider policies, financial institutions, telecommunications providers, legal restrictions and factors outside our control.

Tracing information, reviewing evidence, preparing a report or submitting a request to a provider does not mean funds or assets have been recovered. We will not promise a result simply to persuade you to pay.

Protect your information

We do not need your passwords or secret codes

Never share your password, M-Pesa PIN, banking PIN, one-time password (OTP), cryptocurrency seed phrase or private key. Do not give someone remote access to your device or account just because they claim they can recover it.

Use the official portal for case-specific information. Share only evidence relevant to your case, and remove unnecessary personal or financial details where possible. If an account or payment method may be compromised, contact the provider through its official app, website or published support channel promptly.

Need clarification?

Ask before you decide.

We can clarify the process, proposed scope, fees and limitations. You never need to provide a password, PIN, OTP or seed phrase just to ask a question.
