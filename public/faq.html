Manlung Recovery · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about recovery cases, evidence, calls, payments, privacy and account safety.

01

Cases & recovery

Everything about submitting, tracking and managing a recovery case.

How do I track my case?

Sign in to the client portal to view your case status, timeline, messages and available actions. Case information is shown only to authorized users.

How do I submit a recovery request?

Use New Recovery Request, describe what happened, provide the relevant details and submit the request. Avoid putting passwords, OTPs or recovery codes in the description.

What happens after I submit a case?

Your request is recorded for review. An authorized team member can assess the information, update the case status, request clarification and document actions in the case timeline.

Can I reopen a closed case?

Where reopening is supported, an authorized investigator or owner can reopen the case and the action can be recorded in the case history.

What do the case statuses mean?

Status labels show the current stage of handling, such as pending review, accepted, investigation, awaiting your response, evidence collected, recovery successful, recovered by police/owner, closed or rejected.

Can I update my case after submitting it?

If the portal provides an update or messaging action for your case, use that authenticated channel. Keep new information relevant to the case and avoid sending secrets.

02

Evidence & uploads

How to provide useful evidence while protecting sensitive information.

What should I upload?

Upload only evidence relevant to your case, such as screenshots, receipts, device details or other requested material. Check the portal's allowed file types and limits before uploading.

What should I never upload?

Never upload passwords, PINs, OTPs, authenticator recovery codes, private keys, API keys or other credentials. If a credential is compromised, change or revoke it instead of sending it to support.

Can I upload screenshots?

Yes, when they are relevant to the case. Review screenshots first and remove unrelated personal information or credentials that are not needed for investigation.

Why was my file rejected?

Files can be rejected when their type, size or security validation does not meet portal requirements. Try an accepted format or contact human support if the problem continues.

How is evidence handled?

Evidence should be handled through authenticated case workflows with access controls. Keep your own original copies and do not alter evidence that may be important to an investigation.

03

Calls & human support

What happens when you request a live administrator.

How are calls handled?

Call Admin uses browser-based WebRTC communication. The request can be offered to available administrators, while the call session records the necessary status and connection events.

How many admins can handle one call?

One customer call is assigned to one administrator at a time. Other administrators may be notified or see the request, but once an admin accepts it the session is locked to that assignment.

What if all admins are busy?

Your request can enter the waiting queue instead of interrupting an active call. The queue is handled in order as administrators become available.

Can I leave the waiting queue?

Yes, when the queue controls are available, you can leave the request instead of continuing to wait. You can submit another request later if you still need assistance.

What if my call disconnects?

The call session can detect connection changes and attempt the supported reconnection flow. If reconnection does not succeed, use the portal or human-support channel to continue assistance.

04

Payments & donations

Understand payment status and donation confirmation.

How are donations confirmed?

Donation confirmation should come from server-side payment verification or the configured payment provider callback. A pending or failed transaction is not treated as a confirmed donation.

Why is my payment still pending?

A payment can remain pending while the transaction is being verified. Avoid making repeated payments until you know the status of the original transaction.

What if I was charged but the portal did not update?

Keep the transaction reference and contact support through the official portal. Do not send your PIN, OTP or full authentication credentials to anyone claiming to verify the payment.

Is donating required to open a case?

A donation is separate from the recovery-case workflow unless a specific service page clearly states otherwise. Follow the requirements shown in the authenticated request process.

05

AI, privacy & data

What Manlung AI can do and how to protect your information.

Does the AI know my private case?

The assistant should only access case information through authorized application workflows. It should not disclose another user's information and should never require your password or security codes.

Should I give Manlung AI my password?

No. Never provide passwords, PINs, OTPs, recovery codes, API keys or private keys to the AI or to support staff.

What personal information should I share?

Provide only information needed to identify and understand your recovery issue. Minimize unrelated personal information and redact sensitive details whenever they are not necessary.

Can AI make decisions about my case?

AI can help explain workflows and organize support information, but important case actions should remain subject to the portal's authorization and human administrative processes.

Can I request human support instead?

Yes. Use the portal's human-support or Call Admin options when available, especially for account-specific issues or situations that require human review.

06

Account security

Simple steps to keep your account and recovery information safe.

What if I suspect account abuse?

Stop sharing credentials, change compromised passwords, revoke exposed sessions or tokens where possible, and contact human support through an official Manlung Recovery channel.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious message?

Do not click unknown links or disclose codes. Verify that the request came through an official Manlung Recovery channel and report suspicious activity through support.

Does Manlung Recovery ask for OTPs or passwords?

No legitimate support workflow should require you to disclose your password, PIN, OTP or recovery codes. Treat anyone requesting them as suspicious.

How can I make my account safer?

Use a strong unique password, enable available multi-factor authentication, keep your recovery information private, sign out of untrusted sessions and keep your device and browser updated.

What if I lose access to my account?

Use the official account-recovery process or contact human support. Be ready to provide appropriate verification information, but never send your password or one-time authentication codes.

07

Portal & general

Quick answers for using the Manlung Recovery website.

Which browser should I use?

Use a current version of a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Keep the browser updated for security and WebRTC compatibility.

Why am I being logged out?

Sessions can expire or be revoked as a security measure. Sign in again through the official portal. If unexpected logout continues, contact support.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. The portal is designed to work responsively on phones and computers. For calls, allow the browser microphone permission when prompted.

How do I contact Manlung Recovery?

Use the official Contact page, client portal or supported human-support channel. Verify the website address before entering account information.

Where can I read the legal and privacy information?

See the Privacy Notice and Terms & Conditions linked in the footer and website navigation.
