Frequently asked questions

How do I track my case?

Sign in to the client portal. Your case status, timeline, messages and available investigator actions are protected by account authorization.

What should I upload?

Only relevant evidence. Supported uploads are validated for type and file signature. Do not upload passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, API keys or other secrets.

Can I reopen a closed case?

Eligible completed cases can be reopened by an authorized investigator or owner. The reopen action is recorded in the case timeline.

How are calls handled?

Calls use browser WebRTC signaling. Ringing, acceptance, missed calls, reconnection diagnostics and end state can be recorded for authorized participants.

How are donations confirmed?

Payment confirmation is based on server-side verification/webhook processing. Pending and failed payments are not counted as confirmed donations.

Does the AI know my private case?

Only authenticated, authorized case context may be retrieved. The assistant should not reveal another user's case information and should never ask for credentials or secrets.

What if I suspect account abuse?

Stop sharing secrets, sign out of other sessions from your security controls when available, and contact human support through the portal.
